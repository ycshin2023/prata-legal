Support

Questions, bugs or feedback about Prata Lang? Email hello@pratalang.com and you'll get a reply.

Common questions

Some lessons say "audio soon"

The Korean course is brand new and its audio is still being recorded. The lessons, vocabulary and translations all work; only the spoken audio is pending.

Can I change the app's language?

Yes — Profile → App language. Prata Lang's interface is available in English, Swedish, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, Chinese, German and French, and follows your phone by default.

How do I switch what I'm learning?

Tap the animal on the Home screen — the moose, shiba or tiger — and pick a course.

Deleting your account

Profile → Sign out removes the app's local data. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email the address above and it will be erased.
